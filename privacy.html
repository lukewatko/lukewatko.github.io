Privacy Policy

Agentic Bots is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account.

It reads email headers and short previews to identify marketing senders, and sends unsubscribe requests on the owner's behalf. Data stays on the owner's computer, except that sender names, subjects and previews are sent to Anthropic's Claude to classify senders. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
